What counts as what — so the box isn't a matter of mood.
The through-line: we did something = a little; the other side did something = a big win. Effort is little, commitment is big. If a week genuinely sits between two tiers, take the lower one and say why in the sentence — that's what the sentence is for.
How this works. One row per pillar per week in the Progress tab of the goals Sheet — week | pillar | state | note | priority | who | when. Saving overwrites that week's row for that pillar, so a correction replaces rather than duplicates. Nothing is ever computed or inferred: a blank week is a week nobody read.
The justification is the point. The checkbox is a headline; the sentence is the record. "Big win" with no sentence is worth nothing three months from now, so the save button stays off until there's a sentence.
